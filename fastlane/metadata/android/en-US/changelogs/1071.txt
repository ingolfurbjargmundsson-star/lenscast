Since 0.1.6:
• UnifiedPush: detection alerts can now reach a paired Android receiver through a UnifiedPush distributor - opt-in, end-to-end encrypted, and independent of browser Web Push
• The web server now stays foreground between streams, so alert delivery is not blocked by Android's background-network firewall (adds only the normal FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE permission)
• HTTPS: toggling no longer fails with address-in-use, open dashboards follow the switch to the new scheme and port automatically, and the certificate keeps a stable identity across restarts
• Localization: lens chips in all seven languages, telephoto lenses show their zoom ratio ("3x"), and the lens API exposes kind/zoomRatio
• Security: solid-js 1.9.17 clears the seroval Dependabot alerts, and source-map-js is updated to 1.2.2
